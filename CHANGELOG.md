# Softcover changelog

What changed in each release of Softcover, newest first.

## 3.2.2: What's coming next

*2026-10-18*

The Trending widget now looks ahead, and every widget is ready the moment you place it.

- Trending can show what's coming instead: the 10 most-wanted books releasing in the next month, 3 months, 6 months or year
- New widgets fill in within a second of placing them, and their arrows respond straight away
- The Currently Reading progress bar no longer goes missing, and the percentage stays put
- When Hardcover can't be reached, Trending and Quote of the day say so and offer a refresh button
- Widget covers take far less space on your phone, and are cleared when you remove your last widget or sign out
- Trending's rankings line up, with 10 on a single line, Random pick's cover fills its space, and long quotes stay clear of the quotation mark
- Author names with stray spaces now read cleanly

## 3.2.1: Widgets you can rely on

*2026-10-17*

A fix-up release for the new widgets, so they keep up with you instead of getting in the way.

- Widgets no longer break into "Can't show content" when you page, shuffle or come back from another app, and the arrows always respond
- Covers on Currently Reading and Random pick are drawn at the size they're shown, so they stay sharp
- Long quotes shrink to fit the Quote of the day widget instead of being cut off
- Widgets show a loader while they start or switch to a new shelf, put the title up first, and keep the cover's shape while it loads
- A Random pick set to a list that's no longer on your device says so, and a tap takes you to its settings
- Share sits right in the book page's top bar, no longer tucked under the edit menu

## 3.2.0: On your home screen

*2026-10-17*

Your reading now sits on your home screen too, with four widgets on Android and a place to keep the lines worth remembering.

- Four new widgets on Android: Currently Reading (log progress without opening the app), Trending, Random pick and Quote of the day, all gathered on a new Widgets page in Settings
- Save quotes from a book on its Yours tab; they sync with Hardcover
- Book detail now keeps every read-through under Your reads, and your pace and finish forecast follow the current one
- A new Paused status for the books you mean to come back to
- A Dim theme joins Light and Dark, alongside four new palettes: Vellum, Ink, Foxed and Sea
- Read What's new for each release, follow the Roadmap and find the About page, all inside Settings
- A redesigned share card set over the book's blurred cover, with a choice of tags and length
- Removed a book by mistake? Undo it straight from the snackbar
- Book detail shows a book's other tags too, a couple more lines with each Show more
- Shelving a book keeps the edition you were looking at
- The scanner opens straight to the camera, and you can type an ISBN from the edition sheet
- Smoother motion throughout, with covers and titles that carry into book detail and support for Android's predictive back
- List changes made offline wait for the connection instead of failing

## 3.1.3: Steadier under load
*2026-08-24*

A small release that keeps Softcover on good terms with Hardcover's servers.

- Softcover now paces its requests to the new API rate limits, so busy moments no longer end in errors
- The deadline date picker no longer slips a day in some time zones

## 3.1.2: Every tag, accounted for
*2026-07-24*

- Books with a long list of tags now show all of them, not just the first few

## 3.1.1: Shelves you can swipe
*2026-07-23*

Swipe between the shelves in your Library again, with the neighbouring shelves named above your books.

- Turn shelf swiping on under Settings, Appearance (off by default, on phone and tablet)
- The card at the top of Explore now says what it is: the most anticipated book of the next 30 days, and how many readers are waiting on it
- Back now clears an Explore search before leaving the screen, and Esc does the same on desktop
- When tagging a book, "Show more" opens every tag you've used in that category
- A new "Hide untagged authors" switch on your Profile stats keeps "Who you read" to the authors it has details for
- The genres you read most now show as a share of your books rather than of your tags
- Deadlines show the target date and pages a day again, on your Library list and the Reading screen
