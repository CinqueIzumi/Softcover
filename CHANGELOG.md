# Softcover changelog

What changed in each release of Softcover, newest first.

## 3.1.3: Steadier under load
*2026-08-24*

A small release that keeps Softcover on good terms with Hardcover's servers.

- Softcover now paces its requests to the new API rate limits, so busy moments no longer end in errors
- The deadline date picker no longer slips a day in some time zones

## 3.1.2: Every tag, accounted for
*2026-07-24*

- Books with a long list of tags now show all of them, not just the first few

## 3.1.1: Shelves you can swipe
*2026-07-23*

Swipe between the shelves in your Library again, with the neighbouring shelves named above your books.

- Turn shelf swiping on under Settings, Appearance (off by default, on phone and tablet)
- The card at the top of Explore now says what it is: the most anticipated book of the next 30 days, and how many readers are waiting on it
- Back now clears an Explore search before leaving the screen, and Esc does the same on desktop
- When tagging a book, "Show more" opens every tag you've used in that category
- A new "Hide untagged authors" switch on your Profile stats keeps "Who you read" to the authors it has details for
- The genres you read most now show as a share of your books rather than of your tags
- Deadlines show the target date and pages a day again, on your Library list and the Reading screen
