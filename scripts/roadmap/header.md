# Softcover roadmap

What we're building next for [Softcover](https://github.com/CinqueIzumi/Softcover), grouped by the version it's planned to land in. This is the public view of our plan.

**Current release: {{CURRENT_VERSION}}.** Softcover runs on Android, iPhone, iPad and desktop. 3.2.3 lets the Trending widget show what just came out as well as what's coming soon, and has every widget make full use of the space you give it, on top of 3.2.0's home-screen widgets, synced quotes and a record of every read-through.

A few honest caveats:
- **These are plans, not promises.** Versions are listed in the order we intend to ship them, but order can shift and some items may move, merge, or change shape.
- **No release dates.** We ship when each drop is ready. The version a feature sits under tells you roughly *how soon*, not *when*.
- Every release also includes bug fixes and polish that aren't listed here individually.
