# Components — Share

## Parked for KDoc (Step 07d)

### Share card

`ShareCard` is the single composable that renders any user-facing share artefact (book, hero stat, pulled quote, year-recap slide) into a fixed-aspect editorial composition with a `SOFTCOVER` folio sign-off. It lives in `core:component`, `share/`, and is the contract's reference implementation (component-contract.md § 7.3). Variants are picked by `ShareCardUiModel` subtype: book (cover + title + byline + optional rating + optional quote), reading-update (a *personalised* card attributing the book to the reader — see below), stat (`primary`-filled hero with eyebrow + oversize numeral + caption), quote (oversize low-alpha `quoteGlyph` behind italic headline + primary byline), year-recap (eyebrow + display + bulleted highlights), reading-life (a whole-history recap card — see below). Dimensions live in `ShareCardDimensions`; never override them at the call site.

The **reading-update** variant (`ReadingUpdateShareCardUiModel`) is the centred-stacked personal card: status eyebrow → cover → title → byline → reader identity (circular `RhaydusShimmerImage` avatar + username) → the reader's own tags (when any) → state-specific tail. The reader's tags render as a centred wrapping `FlowRow` of read-only `Chip`s beneath the identity; **spoiler-flagged user tags are excluded** from the card (a share image is public, so a tag the reader hid from other readers must not leak) — the call-site mapper drops them before handing the names to `ShareCard`. It carries two states, keyed off the user's shelf status, and deliberately omits any recency wording (the user may share long after the fact): **finished** (`Read`) uses the eyebrow "FROM MY SHELF" and shows the reader's rating as read-only `StarRatingInput` gold stars plus their review, rendered through the shared `RichText` so inline spoiler spans are masked as redaction blocks exactly as on the book-detail review (the whole-review render is *not* gated — only the explicitly-marked spans are concealed); **reading** (`Reading`) uses "CURRENTLY READING" and shows a progress label (`page X / Y · NN%`, text only — no bar). The card-content mapping lives at the call site (the review travels as a structured `RichTextUiModel`, mapped from the stored `ReviewDocument` by `:core:uibinding`'s `toRichTextUiModel()`; the rating is the stored 0–5 value handed over untouched; the progress label mirrors the book-detail block — time (`listened / total`) for audiobooks, pages for print — with the percentage derived from the matching unit counts when known and falling back to `UserBookRead.progress`, which is already a 0–100 value); `ShareCard` only renders. It is offered alongside the editorial book card via a `SingleChoiceSegmentedButtonRow` toggle ("My update" / "Book") inside the share sheet, shown only when the book is on the reader's Read or Reading shelf — off-shelf books fall back to the book card with no toggle.

Pair with the foundation `CapturableShareCard` + `rememberShareCardCapture(config)` to render the card — Softcover passes `softcoverShareCardCaptureConfig` (gallery album `Softcover`, file-name prefix `softcover`, the app's `FileProvider` authority) and wraps its own public `ShareCard` in `CapturableShareCard`'s generic slot (the card design stays app-side; only the capture plumbing is the foundation's). The foundation `ShareCardCapture` seam (`nl.rhaydus.designsystem.share`, a `commonMain` interface with Android, iOS, and desktop actuals) owns three actions: `capture.saveToGallery(displayName)` writes a PNG into the device gallery (`Pictures/Softcover/` via MediaStore on API 29+, legacy public-Pictures dir + scan on API 26–28; the iOS gallery save is not yet wired); `capture.saveToCache(displayName)` writes a PNG into app cache and returns its identifier; and `capture.share(displayName)` presents the **platform share sheet** for the card image — the OS-level share is part of the capture contract, *not* the calling surface's job (Android builds an `ACTION_SEND` chooser from a `FileProvider content://` URI, iOS presents a `UIActivityViewController`; desktop has no system share sheet, so it copies the PNG to the system clipboard, and its `saveToGallery` opens a native file-save dialog). A surface that just wants to share the card calls `capture.share(...)` and branches on the returned `ShareOutcome` (`Shared` / `Cancelled` / `Failure` — a user-dismissed share sheet is `Cancelled`, treated as a no-op); it never constructs an intent itself. Legacy storage permission (`WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `maxSdkVersion=28`) is requested through `rememberGalleryWritePermissionRequester` — on API 29+ the requester is a synchronous pass-through. Reach for this rather than building a new share image inline; one renderer, one editorial register, one save path.

The **reading-life** variant (`ReadingLifeShareCardUiModel`, `ShareCardDimensions.ReadingLife` — width 300dp, no fixed height, 560dp minimum, 24dp padding) is the `primary`-filled whole-history recap card: a top masthead (`SOFTCOVER` tracking-wide + italic "A reading life" at 0.7 alpha) over a mini scalloped portrait (a 52dp `MaterialShapes.Cookie12Sided` clip with a 3dp `onPrimary` rim, falling back to initials on a translucent `onPrimary` fill when there is no avatar) and the reader's name, then the "TOTAL PAGES READ" hero numeral (`statHero` scaled to 58sp) with a caption that reads "across N books", a **"WHAT YOU READ MOST" genre ranking** placed directly beneath that hero (see below), a "THE READING YEAR" pages-by-month **ridgeline** (a `Canvas` polyline over its own translucent area fill, both drawn in `onPrimary` at stepping alpha — 0.9 line / 0.22 area — with `JAN`–`DEC` labels beneath; the twelve-value input is padded/truncated so a partial history still renders a flat baseline instead of a malformed chart), and a three-stat footer (avg rating — paired with read-only `StarRatingInput` gold stars per the "gold is rating-stars only" rule, day streak, tracked years). Hairline dividers between the ridgeline/genres/footer sections are `onPrimary` at 0.25 alpha. The **genre ranking** (`ReadingLifeGenre` — name + whole-number percentage, rounded at the call site) is the card's second headline and therefore sits immediately under the pages hero, *above* the reading-year chart: an eyebrow over one row per genre, name left and share right, with the leading genre stepped up to `headlineSmall` at full alpha and the runners-up at `body` / 0.78 alpha, so the top genre is legible at poster distance. The whole section is omitted (with its divider) when the reader has no genres yet. Percentages are shares of the reader's genre-tagged *books* (matching the Profile section the card recaps) — a book carries several genres, so they overlap and intentionally do not sum to 100; never add a total or an "everything else" remainder row to this section. The content model is a plain primitive shape (no `core:profile` dependency, so the card stays self-contained in `core:component`); the profile feature maps its `ReadingLife` domain model onto it at the call site.
