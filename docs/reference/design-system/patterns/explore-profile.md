# Patterns — Explore & Profile

### Profile screen

Profile's "reading life" reads top-to-bottom: the reader's portrait/name/bio header, "Reading atlas" (the hero pages-read card + volumes/avg-rating/streak tiles), a **share entry row** (a `surfaceContainer` card — primary glyph tile, two-line prompt, trailing chevron — opening the share sheet below), "The shape of the years" (the peak-annotated bar-column chart, components.md, behind a Books/Pages `SingleChoiceSegmentedButtonRow` toggle that is pure composable-local state, not a TOAD action), "The genres you read most" (the genre proportion stack, components.md), "Who you read" (the author-representation section: a gender proportion stack plus BIPOC/LGBTQ+ three-way Yes/No/Unknown bars, components.md, under a persisted "Hide untagged authors" switch that renormalises every bar over its own tagged authors — the section's only control, and the only toggle anywhere in the reading-life spread), "How you score" (the half-star ratings histogram, components.md, under a headline/caption pair chosen from the reader's classified rating band — never from the bare average), "Recently loved" (up to three real 4★+ reads, cover + title + author + `rating★ · month`), and the demoted **account foot** (a tonal Log out button over a quiet "— · —" colophon). The five new sections (year chart, genre stack, author representation, ratings, recently loved) each open with the standard eyebrow + italic headline pair (§3.2); the reader header and "Reading atlas" keep their eyebrow-only `SectionLabel` (no headline) as a deliberate exception, not a second register. The hero pages card remains the screen's one primary-filled surface (§2.1).

### Profile share sheet

The reading-life share sheet (`AdaptiveModalSheet`, §3.5): a mini accent-bar + eyebrow ("SHARE YOUR YEAR") + a prose line, then a scaled, non-interactive preview of the exportable **reading-life share card** (components.md Share card) centred in the sheet, then an actions row — a primary "Save image" pill (`RhaydusButton`, flex) beside a circular `surfaceContainerHigh` share button (`DesktopTooltip`-wrapped, since it is icon-only) — both driving the foundation `ShareCardCapture` seam (`capture.saveToGallery` / `capture.share`) exactly as the book-detail share sheet does. Opened from two places that must stay in sync: the top bar's share glyph action and the share entry row card; both simply flip the same composable-local sheet-visibility boolean, no TOAD action or event.

### Log-out confirm sheet

A destructive-confirm `AdaptiveModalSheet` (§3.5) that gates the account foot's Log out button — Profile's Log out is demoted to plumbing (the Settings menu row, components/navigation.md, is the sibling convention: navigational chrome, never a headline action), so it never fires directly. Anatomy: an italic `headlineMedium` "Leaving so soon?" over a short prose line, a primary `RhaydusButton` "Log out" pill that dispatches the existing log-out action and then dismisses, and a text "Stay" affordance that just dismisses. No new TOAD state — the sheet's own visibility is composable-local, and "Log out" wires straight through to the screen's existing log-out action.

### Title-page sheet (Create list)

A centred, single-field modal composition for a surface so small that the usual eyebrow-plus-headline sheet header would outweigh its own content — currently the create-list sheet, "List Creation 1a." Anatomy, top to bottom, all centred: a bar-less `eyebrow` ("A NEW LIST") — the deliberate §2.3 exception, since the rule below already opens the region; a full-width hairline broken in the middle by a small `quoteGlyph` at the `primary` @ 0.28 tint exception (§2.2); a borderless, label-less name hero at display scale, carrying the platform text cursor tinted `primary` and a real-title placeholder (not an instructional label — the placeholder shows the reader what a good entry looks like, e.g. "Summer reads" rather than "List name"); an inset (not full-bleed) hairline divider; the privacy prose toggle sentence (see "Prose toggle", editorial.md); and a single full-width filled `RhaydusButton` pill as the only primary action. There is no separate headline — the name field itself carries that weight, which is what makes this composition an exception rather than a shorter version of the canonical header (§3.5). Reach for this pattern only when a sheet's entire job is naming or capturing one short piece of text as an act of authorship; a sheet with more than one field, or a field alongside settings/options, wants the canonical `EditorialSectionHeader` opening instead.

The hero is **sized to its own characters**: it holds one line and steps its type size down to fit the width available, rather than wrapping or clipping. Three consequences are load-bearing, not incidental. The placeholder must sit *beside* the input rather than stacked behind it — a centred placeholder overlaid on a centred empty field puts the caret in the middle of the placeholder word. It must share the hero's fitted size and stay single-line, or it wraps to a height the single-line field does not have and the caret drifts off the text entirely. And the caret must sit at the placeholder's **leading** edge, not its trailing one: the placeholder is a hint the first keystroke replaces wholesale, so a caret after it promises that typing appends to the example. A caret anywhere but where the next character will land is the tell that this pattern has been rebuilt as a plain centred text field with a placeholder layered over it.
